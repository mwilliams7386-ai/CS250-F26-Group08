# Group Contract

### How work will be divided:
We will have collectively decide who wants to do each part of the project. Afterwards a project manager will assign each person approximately equal work, including themselves, a portion of the project to manage. Each person will work on files or individually so there are no conflicts in commits. Towards the end, if anyone needs assistance or any files need to be edited according to the project manager or another project member, then that work will be assigned to the original member if possible. If that person cannot finish their portion, it will be given to the first person to finish their portion. 

### How group members will communicate:
We have a discord server that has several channels. This can be used to manage workflow and ensure everyone is able to communicate freely. Additionally, there is a call to discuss over if necessary. 

### Expected response times:
Everyone should be able to respond within 24 hours, and all projects are expected to be submitted on the agreed due date. 

### How deadlines will be handled:
Everyone will submit their portion on an agreed upon date. This date will generally be 2-3 days before the final project submission. Afterwards we will edit and fix sections before having the final submission on the assignment deadline. 

### What happens if someone cannot complete their assigned work:
Other group members will either trade work to assign an easier portion to the lacking group member, or another group member can work with the lacking member to assist them in finishing their workload. 

### How disagreements will be resolved:
Murder whoever disagrees with Gabe. 

### When/how you will meet outside of class if necessary:
We will meet virtually through discord on times that are agreed upon at the beginning of the project. 

### Who will be responsible for final submissions:
The project manager will be responsible for the final submission.
