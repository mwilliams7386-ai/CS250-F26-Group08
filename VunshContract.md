# Group Contract


## How work will be divided
```Everyone should be doing an equal share of work, and equal can be defined by group in each context. ```

## How group members will communicate
```Through Discord, Imessage, or in person communications```

## Expected response times
```Most of us can respond within the hour```

## How deadlines will be handled
```We will divide up the work into a timeline and make sure we have everything done in incremental phases, until the final project is done on time.```

## What happens if someone cannot complete their assigned work
```We will figure out how we can fairly make it up across the group```

## How disagreements will be resolved
```We will communicate effectively with the members in a way that allows us to resolve issues without getting personal.```

## When/how you will meet outside of class if necessary
```We will arrange times to meet outside of class, and we already share many other classes together.```

## Who will be responsible for final submissions
```We can determine who wants to submit the final submission when it is time to submit something.```
